The **m8 (m4×2)** is an 8-key hardware development testbed designed to validate scanning a switch matrix via chained **`74HC595`** (column output) and buffered **`74HC589`** (row input) shift registers over hardware SPI while sharing the SPI bus with a **WeAct SSD1680 e-ink display**, alongside an **$\text{I}^2\text{C}$ OLED screen** and a **clickable rotary encoder**.

The circuitry validated on this board directly informed the architecture of [**m65 Rev 10/11**](m65.md) and [**m66 Rev 2/3**](m66.md). See [Not Enough Pins?](notenoughpins.md) for the full schematic analysis of this SPI bus-sharing circuit.
